# Shiplight Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

Shiplight is an iOS app for reviewing Bitbucket Cloud pull requests.

**What the app stores.** Your Atlassian account email, the API token you create, and the workspace slug you enter. These are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only: not synced to iCloud Keychain and not included in device backups. Pull request data you view is cached on the device, excluded from backups, so the app opens quickly.

**Where data goes.** Credentials and requests are sent only to `api.bitbucket.org` over HTTPS, to perform the actions you take in the app (listing, approving, commenting on and merging pull requests). User avatars are loaded from Atlassian's image servers. Images embedded by pull request authors in descriptions or comments are loaded from the HTTPS URL they provided. "Open in Bitbucket" and build-status links open in Safari.

**What the app does not do.** Shiplight contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

**Removing your data.** Sign out removes the stored credentials and cached data. Deleting the app removes cached data; the saved token is removed on the next fresh install, or when you sign out.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/mrhe07/shiplight-privacy/issues.

Shiplight is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Atlassian. Bitbucket is a trademark of Atlassian Pty Ltd.
